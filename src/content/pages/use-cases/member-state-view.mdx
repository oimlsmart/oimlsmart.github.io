officer opens the registry the way she opens any official record: which
authority carries her country's designations, with what scopes; which
laboratories associate to it; what the utilizers may see. The
difference is that the chain is data, not a filing cabinet: every
certificate her regime relies on answers to it, and anyone, her
inspectors included, can verify against it without an account and
without a phone call.

## The story

The officer's quarterly review, as the platform runs it. She starts at
the participant registry: her country's Issuing Authority entry, its
scopes per Recommendation, the associated Test Laboratories, kept as
registry data the scheme maintains, not as letters in a drawer. She
follows the authority's issued work where it actually lives: the public
register, where every certificate the chain issues publishes as the
validity reference. She checks one the way her market inspectors would:
the number into the verify page, the verdict back from the registered
copy, the live revocation and suspension lists consulted in the same
answer. She onboards a new colleague through the identity service:
accounts per organization, approved by her own administrator, the real
member directory selectable. And the utilizers her country designates
see exactly what their designation opens, the reports and certificates
their access covers, honestly scoped, never broader.

## What this changes, and what it does not

Today the chain lives in designations letters, scope annexes, and the
register's periodic publications; following it means asking, and
verifying means phoning the authority that issued. On the platform the
chain is data and the register answers continuously. What does not
change: your legal responsibility, your designations, your custody. The
member state proposes and designates exactly as before; the platform
keeps the chain readable.

## The honest questions

### Does our data leave our custody?

No. The self-host entitlement attaches to your category exactly so
custody never has to move: your own instance, your data residency, the
streaming update channel. The hosted official platform exists for the
members who choose it; the architecture never requires it. The
[deployment matrix](/use-cases/deployment-modes) renders the postures
plain.

### Does the platform decide anything legally ours?

No. Designation, acceptance, issuance, withdrawal stay exactly where
your law puts them; the platform computes verdicts and records who
decided what. The liability surface is your existing one, with better
evidence.

### What does adopting this commit us to?

Reading costs nothing (the public surfaces are public), participating
costs accounts (issued per organization, approved by your own
administrator), and self-hosting is one service-class deployment with
the rolling update channel. Nothing is retired until your regime is
satisfied; the text of every Recommendation stays.

### Our market relies on certificates from other member states. What changes for them?

The verify page answers from the registered copy regardless of which
authority issued, by number or by file, and the certificate downloads
as CNML, as a Verifiable Credential, and as an AAS submodel. The
[interop positions](/technologies/dataspace-interop) are public and
boring on purpose.
