## The story, chapter by chapter

The five demo flows are not five stories; they are one story told from
five chairs. The per-role chapters (the applicant's, the authority's,
the laboratory's) each have their own page on this site, linked as the
arc passes their chair. The thread that ties the chapters together on
the demo is the canonical worked example: the ACME LC-500 load cell's
application, its evaluation project, and certificate
R60/2021-A-EX1-26.01.

### Chapter 1 · The application (flow 01)

**Start the application; the wizard's first question is the
Recommendation.** No silent default: the applicant picks R 60 (or R 91,
R 129, R 144) from cards, then the instrument step derives from that
Recommendation's model, the samples and documentation follow, and the
Issuing Authority is picked from cards that only show authorities able
to take the selected Recommendation. The draft saves; the steps are
individually navigable. Sign in with the one-click **Applicant** demo
account and [open the wizard](https://demo.oimlsmart.org/app/portal/applications/new).

**Follow the application's journey from the first minute.** The
applicant's own page carries the six-stage journey with the promise set
and per-claim verification status; the notifications arrive at every
stage. This is flow 05 running underneath all the others.

### Chapter 3 · The laboratory's work (flow 03)

**The test request lands in the laboratory's inbox.** The laboratory
reviews, rejects with reason or accepts, and accepting joins it to the
Evaluation Project's dataspace. Sign in with the one-click **Test
Laboratory** account for the [inbox](https://demo.oimlsmart.org/app/lab/).

Then the substantial work: each assigned test runs model-driven, the
procedure, its parameters, and its acceptance criteria all from the
Recommendation's model, the measured values judged against the limits
the model computes. The test report is authored on the model's own form
and submitted to the authority through the project, as data, not as a
PDF. The laboratory's chair has its own page: [For Test
Laboratories](/about/audiences/laboratories).

### Chapter 4 · The evaluation and the certificate (flow 04)

**The authority validates the evidence and decides.** Every test-report
row validates against the model's acceptance rules; the verdicts are
computed, never hand-tallied; the review period's comments resolve
before finalization. The certificates desk then lists what is ready:
the certified scope comes from the evaluation's verdicts, and the
signature is the officer's own attributed act.

## What this changes, and what it does not

Today this arc runs on email and document stores: the application as a
scanned form, the test schedule as a spreadsheet, the reports as PDFs,
the type evaluation report assembled by hand, and progress tracked by
asking. On the platform every stage is an act with a record, the forms
derive from the Recommendation's model, and the certificate carries its
evidence chain. What does not change: the authority's review,
evaluation, decision, and signature; the laboratory's judgment at the
bench; the applicant's responsibility for the instrument. The verdict
engine computes; the people decide.

## The honest questions

### Is this a real workflow or a mock-up?

Every capture on this page was taken on the live demo by the scripted
apparatus on the date the caption names; each figure links the exact
surface it came from. The demo runs the same software as the member
instances, in demo posture, against a fictional cast (the ACME load
cell, the example authorities and laboratories), and it resets nightly.
The guided-demo entry on the login page walks the same arc with a
presenter script.

### Does the platform decide the outcome?

No. The verdict engine computes the Recommendation's arithmetic and
states what the evidence supports, including INDETERMINATE where
evidence is missing. The review, the evaluation, the issuance, and the
signature are the authority's acts, recorded as theirs; the
laboratory's readings and judgments are the laboratory's.

### Our applicants still send paper. Is this all-or-nothing?

No. Records mode exists for the transition: the authority can enter an
application for a client, register an offline evaluation, import
records. What came from paper is marked as coming from paper; the audit
trail never pretends otherwise. Certificates keep their paper meaning
throughout; the CNML original and the public verification are
additions.

### What happens after the certificate issues?

The lifecycle is first-class: annex, revise, replace, renew, suspend,
withdraw, each a recorded act, and the public verification reads the
live status. What today ends at issuance is where the SMART+ vision
continues: [continuous compliance](/use-cases/continuous-compliance)
keeps judging the instrument in service, roadmap-labeled where it is
not yet live.
