A SMART Recommendation is an OIML Recommendation rendered as a structured,
executable model: the same source drives the page you read, the forms the
laboratory fills, and the verdicts the platform computes. The published text
stays authoritative, and the model is its machine-actionable companion, with
every element traceable to the clause it came from.

## Why it exists

A Recommendation as published is prose. Every tool that touches it, the
editor, the test-bench software, the certificate template, re-encodes the
text by hand, and every re-encoding drifts a little from the document the
committee adopted. Two laboratories reading the same clause can derive
two different test procedures, and the divergence surfaces only when the
results are compared.

The model removes the re-encoding. Requirements, conformance tests, and
report forms derive from one instrument model, so the reading, the testing,
and the verdict cannot drift apart.

## How it works

Each Recommendation is authored as a Primmel package: the instrument as a
subject with its anatomy, the requirements as constraints bound to the
subject's aspects, the conformance tests that verify each requirement, and
the report forms whose fields bind into the same graph. Every content
element carries clause-level provenance naming its source document and
clause (for example `urn:oiml:pub:r:60-1:2021`, clause 5.2), so any
rendered statement can be walked back to the adopted text.

The authority stays where the OIML process puts it. The committee's
decision is the text; the model is derived from it and checked against it,
never the reverse. The packages are the single source of truth: the data
trees the platform serves are generated artifacts, and a drift guard proves
both directions byte-clean, so what the platform runs is what was authored.

## How it interops

The model exports to the formats neighboring tooling consumes: ReqIF for
requirements-management systems, RDF/OWL with SHACL shapes for linked-data
consumers, and OpenCDD back-references for attribute definitions. Every
export is a generated projection of the model, never a second source, and
each projection states what it carries and what it leaves behind. The
[Dataspace and interoperability page](/technologies/dataspace-interop)
maps the full set.
