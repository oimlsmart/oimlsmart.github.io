## What the service is

The platform is software tooling for the OIML Certification System (OIML-CS), the system defined by OIML B 18 for issuing, registering, and using OIML certificates and type evaluation reports. The platform records applications, runs structured test and evaluation workflows, computes results from the declared criteria, and formats the reports and certificates the workflow produces.

The platform is the scheme's tooling, not the scheme. Determinations, designations, and certificates remain the acts of the responsible OIML-CS participants: issuing authorities, test laboratories, and the Management Committee, acting under OIML B 18 and the applicable Recommendations. Software output does not override those documents, and a record in the platform is evidence of the workflow, not a substitute for the governing decision.

## The demo instances

Demo instances are clearly marked. They carry fictional data only: fictional instruments, fictional organisations, fictional people, fictional certificates. Nothing on a demo instance produces a real decision, a real certificate, or any real-world effect. Do not enter real data on a demo instance, and do not present demo output as a genuine certification artefact.
