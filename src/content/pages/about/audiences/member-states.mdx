OIML SMART turns the Recommendations your regulations cite into
machine-actionable models, so compliance becomes something your systems
can check, not only something your documents assert. Your state keeps
custody: the platform self-hosts under the Member State entitlement,
and the text of every Recommendation stays.

## What you do today

Your national type-approval regime references OIML Recommendations as
PDF documents. Every assessment under them is a manual exercise;
every test report arrives on paper or as an unstructured file; market
surveillance means sampling instruments in the field and hoping the
sample tells the truth. You carry the legal responsibility for
metrological trust in your economy, with tooling from the last
century.

## What changes

An OIML SMART Recommendation is **executable law-adjacent content**:
requirements are constraints, tests are processes, report forms are
evidence interfaces, all machine-checkable, all carrying clause-level
provenance to the source document your regulation cites. Instruments
can serve **SMART twins**: governed projections of their real state
that answer only the aspects the Recommendation governs. Compliance
becomes **monitorable between assessments**, not only at them.

This is the direction legal metrology was always going to take. The
question is whether it arrives as a coherent international system or as
a thicket of incompatible national portals. OIML SMART is the coherent
one, built on OIML's own documents, with OIML's members as the
governing audience.

## What it looks like in practice

The **NMI adoption program** is a tiered entry, not a leap: read the
position pack (traceability mechanized, reference channels, U:MPE ≤
1:3, pairing windows, the concepts your metrologists already use,
formalized), reproduce a type evaluation end to end against the
simulator with your own tooling, then trial one Recommendation with one
laboratory. Nothing is retired until your regime is satisfied.
