## What this changes, and what it does not

Today your national type-approval regime references OIML
Recommendations as PDF documents; every assessment is manual, every test
report arrives unstructured, and market surveillance samples instruments
and hopes the sample tells the truth. On the platform the Recommendation
is executable content and the register answers. What does not change:
your legal responsibility, your designations, your custody. The text
stays; the model joins it.

## The honest questions

### Sovereignty: does our data leave our custody?

No. The self-host entitlement attaches to your category exactly so
custody never has to move: your own instance, your data residency, the
streaming update channel. The hosted official platform exists for the
members who choose it; the architecture never requires it.

### Liability: does the platform decide anything legally ours?

No. The platform computes verdicts from the Recommendation's model and
records who decided what; the legal acts (designation, acceptance,
issuance, withdrawal) remain exactly where your law puts them. The
liability surface is your existing one, with better evidence.

### Cost: what does adopting this actually commit us to?

The minimal honest answer: reading costs nothing (the public surfaces
are public), participating costs accounts (the identity service issues
them per organization), and self-hosting is one service-class deployment
with the rolling update channel. The adoption program is tiered: read
the position pack, reproduce a type evaluation against the simulator,
then trial one Recommendation with one laboratory. Nothing is retired
until your regime is satisfied.

### Interop: is this a walled garden?

The positions are public and boring on purpose: ISO/IEC SMART, ReqIF,
RDF/SHACL, W3C Verifiable Credentials, DPP, AAS, OpenCDD, DSP/ODRL. The
certificate downloads as CNML, as a VC, and as an AAS submodel today;
the [dataspace and interop page](/technologies/dataspace-interop) names
the planes and the export doctrine.

### Gradualness: what happens to our PDFs and our existing register?

Nothing dies. The text of every Recommendation stays; certificates keep
their paper meaning; records mode lets offline work enter the record
honestly marked. The model joins the text; it does not replace it.
