If your operation depends on measuring instruments, OIML SMART makes
their compliance state something you can query instead of a sticker you
trust. If your organization is a Utilizer or an Associate designated
under the OIML-CS, the platform renders that designation as a fact and
opens the evaluation-report and certificate surfaces it grants.

## What you do today

The instruments your operation depends on, the belt scale at the
quarry, the gas meter at the custody-transfer point, are certified on
a cycle. Between verifications you run on faith: a sticker, a date,
and the hope that nothing drifted. When a dispute arises, you
reconstruct the instrument's behavior from logs and memory. And when
an OIML certificate or type evaluation report lands on your desk as
the basis for a national approval, accepting it means reading a PDF
and trusting its letterhead.

## What changes

A SMART-twin-capable instrument serves its **governed aspects live**:
the quantities the Recommendation cares about, queryable through a
standard interface, certified as faithful to the physical device by the
twin-certification program. Compliance stops being a date on a sticker
and becomes **a stream you can read**, verdicts computed against the
Recommendation's requirements as evidence arrives, accruing append-only
so history is never rewritten. Your own implementation model can
import the manufacturer's reference model as a standing promise, or
plug the live twin directly into your operations monitoring.

For designated bodies the change is just as concrete. A Utilizer or an
Associate signs the Declaration the OIML-CS prescribes (PD-08),
accepting OIML certificates and type evaluation reports as the basis
for national or regional type approvals. On the platform that
designation is a registry fact linked to your designating Member State
or Corresponding Member, your personnel hold accounts against it, and
the report and certificate surfaces open per the designation, with the
evidence chain attached, so acceptance becomes verification instead of
trust.

## What it looks like in practice

Your quarry's belt scale answers queries through its twin interface;
the compliance engine re-judges its requirements on a schedule; you
see the current verdict and the full evidence history behind it. When
the scale drifts out of its maximum permissible errors, the verdict
degrades, visibly, immediately, with the probes that show it, instead
of surfacing at next year's verification as a year of disputed
invoices.

## What you can try now

The three-layer twin is the architecture's central idea: the physical
device, the manufacturer's full twin, and the SMART twin as the
governed projection between them, with the standard itself declaring
what is governed. The twin-cert demo shows a certificate issued for
exactly this fidelity promise.

## Who to talk to

Write to [info@oimlsmart.org](mailto:info@oimlsmart.org), the
programme's front-door address, or use the
[contact page](/about/contact). For accounts, talk to your designating
Member State or Corresponding Member; the designation you hold is what
your access renders. And ask your instrument suppliers the only
question that matters: *where is your SMART twin?*
