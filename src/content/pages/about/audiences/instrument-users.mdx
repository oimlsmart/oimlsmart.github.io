## What you can do

Every act below was performed against the live demo before it was listed;
the dated screenshot under each is the audit artifact.

**Verify any certificate by its number.** The answer comes from the
public register (B 18:2025 §15.8): the particulars, the classifications,
the validity window, and the live revocation and suspension status from
the W3C status lists, with the check's own timestamp.

**Verify the certificate file itself.** A certificate downloaded as a
Verifiable Credential (the .vc.json file) verifies against its issuer's
DID document: the same verdict, straight from the file, no lookup
needed. We downloaded the demo certificate's VC and verified it in the
audit for this page.

**Browse the register.** Every certificate the instance publishes, per
scheme, with the export and the print view; the registered copy is the
validity reference, and a presented document checks against it field by
field.

**Where you are designated, see the designation rendered.** A Utilizer or
Associate signs the Declaration the OIML-CS prescribes (PD-08). On the
platform that designation is a registry fact linked to your designating
member; your personnel hold accounts against it.

**Declare your Additional National Requirements as data.** ANRs are
declared per Recommendation and per market, moderated by the CS
registry, and only an APPROVED declaration is live. Applicants targeting
your market see the requirement before they apply, not in a finding
after.

## What this changes, and what it does not

Today the instruments your operation depends on are certified on a
cycle, and between verifications you run on faith: a sticker, a date,
and the hope that nothing drifted. Accepting a certificate means reading
a PDF and trusting its letterhead. On the platform, acceptance becomes
verification: the register answers in seconds, and the answer carries
the live status. What does not change: your national requirements and
your approvals remain yours; the platform informs them, it does not
pre-empt them.

## The honest questions

### Is the public answer legally the register's answer?

Yes. The verification reads the copy the BIML registered (B 18:2025
§15.8): the registered copy is the validity reference, and the verify
page says so on its face. A mismatch between a presented document and
the registered copy is exactly what the page exists to surface.

### What about the instrument between verifications?

Today, honestly: the platform verifies certificates, not instruments in
the field. The continuous answer (the instrument's twin serving its
governed aspects live, verdicts re-judged as evidence arrives) is the
SMART+ tier and it is roadmap, labeled as such above. We will not claim
it early.

### Our national requirements are law. Who moderates them here?

You declare them; the CS registry moderates the declaration; only an
APPROVED declaration is live. The platform never edits your requirement:
it stores your verbatim text, your instrument's clause reference, and
your scheme scope, and renders them to applicants targeting your market.

### We are not designated under the OIML-CS. Is any of this ours?

The public surfaces are everyone's: verify, the register, the published
Recommendations, the AI service. The designated surfaces open with a
designation; your designating Member State or Corresponding Member is
the path, and the entitlement row above says exactly what it opens.
