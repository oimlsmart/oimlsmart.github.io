OIML SMART is not one product with one user. It is a program that
touches every role in legal metrology — and for each role, it changes
something specific. Pick your seat:

| Audience | What changes for you |
|---|---|
| [Member states & NMIs](/about/audiences/member-states) | national law meets executable standards: monitorable, verifiable, continuously compliant by construction |
| [Manufacturers](/about/audiences/manufacturers) | publish a reference model of your instrument, ship a SMART twin, certify continuously instead of episodically |
| [Test laboratories](/about/audiences/laboratories) | structured test runs, evidence captured once, reports generated — rehearsed on the simulator first |
| [Issuing authorities](/about/audiences/issuing-authorities) | the OIML-CS as a digital workflow: applications to certificates, every verdict computed |
| [Instrument users](/about/audiences/instrument-users) | continuous compliance for the instruments you depend on — query the twin, read the evidence |
| [Educators & students](/about/audiences/educators) | the classroom: certify a simulated load cell before you ever touch a real one |

Each page names the pain as it exists today, the mechanism that removes
it, what the system looks like in practice, the proof you can check
yourself, and the next step to take.
