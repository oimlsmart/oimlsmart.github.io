## The honest questions

### Is any of this live, or is it a rendering?

Live. Every capability screenshot on these pages was captured from the
running demo estate on the date the caption names, by a script
(`scripts/capture-audiences.ts` in this repository) that performs the
act before it photographs it. An act that cannot be performed does not
list.

### Where is the line between SMART and SMART+?

SMART is what you can click today: the Recommendations as models, the
platform, the CNML certificates, the public verification. SMART+ is the
vision: the instrument twin, the simulation, signed measurements,
continuous compliance. The vision items carry the word "roadmap" and a
link to the page that tracks them; nothing future is stated as present.

### We are several of these audiences at once. Where do we start?

Start with the role that hurts most this quarter. The estate is one
account and one login; an Issuing Authority officer and a laboratory
manager in the same organization each get their own console. The demo's
one-click accounts let you sit in every seat before you ask for
anything.
