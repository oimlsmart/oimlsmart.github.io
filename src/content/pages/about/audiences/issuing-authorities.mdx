OIML SMART runs your certification workflow the way B 18 describes it:
applications in, test laboratories dispatched, evaluations synthesized,
certificates signed, every verdict computed from the Recommendation's
model rather than transcribed from prose. Your liability posture does
not widen; the platform does digitally what your authority does today.

## What you do today

Your authority coordinates the certification chain over email and
document stores: applications arrive as PDFs, test reports come back as
PDFs, the type evaluation report is assembled by hand, and the
certificate is a signed artifact whose supporting evidence lives in
three other systems. Accepting another authority's evaluation means
re-reading their documents and trusting their prose.

## What changes

The **OIML-CS is modelled as executable processes** on B 18:2025 and
the ISO/IEC 17000-series chain it normatively references. Applications,
test dispatch, laboratory reports, type evaluation, and issuance are
stages in one workflow with one verdict engine. The certificate that
emerges is a **CNML document**: machine-readable, cryptographically
signed, and, the decisive property, **carrying its evidence chain**.
Every claim in it names the tests, readings, and verdicts behind it;
anyone holding the public keys can verify it without phoning you.

## What it looks like in practice

An application enters through the applicant portal; dispatch goes to a
designated laboratory; evidence accrues as the laboratory works; the
type evaluation report synthesizes verdicts that were computed, not
transcribed; issuance is a signing event over a verdict chain. When
another authority's evaluation crosses your desk, you verify its chain
cryptographically instead of trusting its letterhead. Suspension and
withdrawal semantics are modelled too, a certificate's lifecycle is as
explicit as its issuance.
