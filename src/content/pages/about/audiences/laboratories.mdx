## What you can do

Every act below was performed against the live demo before it was listed;
the dated screenshot under each is the audit artifact.

**Receive the dispatch in your inbox, live.** The IA's TestRequest names
the forms, the samples, and the terms; it appears in your incoming
bucket the moment it is issued, no email to notice.

**Open the request and see exactly your share.** The scope, the samples
with their serials, the issuing authority's context. Accept, or decline
with a reason; silence is not a state the record allows.

## What this changes, and what it does not

Today a type evaluation means: read the procedures from a PDF, set up
each condition by hand, record readings into a form, transcribe the form
into a report, and answer findings about transcription. On the platform
the evidence is captured at the moment of work and the report is
generated from it; where evidence cannot be produced, the verdict reads
INDETERMINATE, never a silent pass. What does not change: your
measurement, your competence, your responsibility for the result. The
platform removes the transcription, not the metrology.

## The honest questions

### Does the workbench replace our metrologists' judgment?

No. The workbench walks the procedure and captures what the operator
does; the measurement, the setup, and the professional call remain the
laboratory's. Where the evidence does not support a verdict, the system
says INDETERMINATE. Your laboratory never has to choose between silence
and a pass.

### We already have a LIMS. Is this another island?

The platform's surfaces are data, not documents: reports, evidence rows,
and dispatches are structured records. Interop is a first-class position
(the standards map: ISO/IEC SMART, ReqIF, RDF/SHACL, W3C VC, AAS), and
records mode lets a report authored in your own systems enter honestly.
The deep LIMS integrations are roadmap work; the honest bridge is live.

### What if our laboratory works for several Issuing Authorities?

The dispatch is one TestRequest per laboratory per engagement; your
inbox shows exactly your share, per IA, and each accepted assignment
joins only that project's dataspace. Cross-authority visibility is the
cone machinery's job, and it is enforced, not configured per project.

### We ran the evaluation before any of this existed. Do we redo it?

No. Register the offline report; it enters marked registered-offline and
the IA reviews it as a real act. Nothing is retroactively digitized, and
nothing claims evidence it does not have.
