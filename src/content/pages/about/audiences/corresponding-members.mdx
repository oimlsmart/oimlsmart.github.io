## What this changes, and what it does not

Today you follow the OIML's work at one remove: publications arrive as
documents, comments travel by correspondence, and the certificate
register is something you consult rather than something that answers
you. On the estate your participation gets an operational surface. What
does not change: your voice. The platform never turns read access into
a vote; the Convention governs participation, and the platform serves
it.

## What you can use, and what you can run

Every hosted service is yours today, including the official OIML-CS
SMART Platform; the hosted path never requires running anything
yourself. The one entitlement that ratification unlocks is self-hosting
the platform software, quoted below from the estate's single
entitlement source, with the honest path upward.

the platform never sells what the Convention governs.

## The honest questions

### Is the hosted platform really full access for us?

Yes, per the estate's entitlement policy: Member States and
Corresponding Members both use the official hosted platform, and the
hosted path never requires the self-host entitlement. The matrix above
is the single source; this page quotes it rather than paraphrasing it.

### What exactly do we gain over the publications we already receive?

The same documents, plus their operational form: the Recommendation as
a model you can navigate by clause, the register that answers lookups,
verification you can perform during a committee meeting, and the AI
service that cites the corpus. The publications remain; the surfaces
join them.

### Does using the hosted estate commit us politically?

No. Using a hosted tool is not a treaty act. Your participation status
is the Convention's business; the estate's job is to make the
information and the evidence equally available to you, which the
entitlement row above states plainly.

### When we ratify, what changes on the estate?

Your category moves to Member State: the self-host entitlement unlocks
(your own instance, your data residency, the streaming update channel),
and your organization gains the right to propose Issuing Authorities.
The upsell panel above is the honest version, and the
[OIML membership page](https://www.oiml.org/en/structure/members) is the
authoritative one.
