A Corresponding Member is a country or economy that has not ratified the
OIML Convention but participates in the OIML's work: it pays an annual
fee, receives the same information and publications as Member States, and
comments on drafts without a vote. On the OIML SMART estate a
Corresponding Member uses every hosted service in full; running the
platform software yourself is the one entitlement that waits for
ratification.

## What you do today

You follow the OIML's work at one remove. Publications arrive as
documents, draft comments travel by correspondence, and the certificate
register is something you consult rather than something that answers
you. If you have designated an Associate under the OIML-CS, its
Declaration and its standing live in files, and checking what an OIML
certificate means for your national approvals means reading PDFs and
writing email.

## What changes

Your participation gets an operational surface. Your organization's
personnel hold accounts on the estate and read what Member States read:
the Recommendations as machine-actionable models with clause-level
provenance, the OIML-CS register, the certificate verification surface.
Your designated Associate appears in the participant registry as what it
is, an organization linked to you by its designation, its Declaration
standing visible instead of filed away. And the estate's AI service
answers your metrologists' questions with citations from the OIML
corpus, at the member tier, by construction.

What does not change: your voice. The platform never turns read access
into a vote; the Convention governs participation, the platform serves
it.

## What you can use, and what you can run

Every hosted service is yours today, including the official OIML-CS
SMART Platform; the hosted path never requires running anything
yourself. The one entitlement that ratification unlocks is self-hosting
the platform software, quoted below from the estate's single
entitlement source, with the honest path upward.

The membership categories themselves are the OIML's, defined by the
Convention and described on the
[OIML membership page](https://www.oiml.org/en/structure/members);
the platform never sells what the Convention governs.

## Who to talk to

Write to [info@oimlsmart.org](mailto:info@oimlsmart.org), the
programme's front-door address, or use the
[contact page](/about/contact). Onboarding a Corresponding Member
organization means accounts for your personnel and your Associate's
designation rendered honestly, nothing to host.
