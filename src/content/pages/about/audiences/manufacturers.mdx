## What this changes, and what it does not

Today a type evaluation is an episodic, expensive event: documentation
by email, samples by courier, then silence until the report arrives, a
photograph of one week in your product's life. On the platform the
application is data from the first step, and the file's progress is
yours to watch. What does not change: the Recommendation's text, the
IA's decision, the legal meaning of the certificate. The model joins the
text; it does not replace it.

## The honest questions

### Does this change how we certify today?

No. The legal instruments are untouched: the Recommendation's text
stays, the IA decides, the certificate means what it meant. What changes
is the mechanics around it: the application is structured data, the
evaluation's progress is visible, and the certificate you receive is
machine-verifiable. PDFs never die; the model joins them.

### Who sees our product data?

Only the parties to your evaluation, per the visibility cones the
platform enforces: your application is addressed to one Issuing
Authority; the test laboratory sees the dispatch it accepted; the BIML
sees what is registered. Your fuller product documentation stays yours;
the platform never publishes your file.

### Is the Verifiable Credential real, or a demo prop?

Real. It verifies against the issuer's DID document on the
[public verify page](https://demo.oimlsmart.org/app/verify/), which is
exactly the check your customers will run. In the audit for this page we
downloaded the VC from the certificate page and verified the file
itself, with no account.

### What does this cost us?

Using the hosted estate costs you no infrastructure: the applicant
portal is a hosted console, and the demo instance is a public sandbox.
The self-host entitlements belong to Member States, not to applicants;
you never run anything.

### We certify families and variants. Does every variant reopen the file?

No. Variants and family members share the model's common spine: the
wizard's instrument step picks the family and the models in scope, and
the evaluation treats the delta, not the whole file again.
