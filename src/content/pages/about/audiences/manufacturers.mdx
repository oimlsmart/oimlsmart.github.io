## The world today

Type evaluation is an episodic, expensive event: prepare documentation,
ship samples, wait for the laboratory, respond to findings, receive a
report that is a photograph of one week in your product's life. Every
variant re-opens the same questions; every market asks them
differently; and once the certificate is framed, neither you nor your
customers can *see* conformity — you can only assert it.

## What changes

You publish a **reference model** of your instrument: its identity,
its holdings, its behavior — every node mapped explicitly onto the
Recommendation's requirements, tests, and forms. Conformity stops being
prose about your product and becomes **a computation over your
product's model**. Then the larger step: ship a **SMART twin** with the
instrument — a governed digital projection that serves exactly the
aspects the Recommendation governs, live, to laboratories, authorities,
and customers. The **twin-certification program** certifies that your
twin stays faithful to the physical device — the promise is declared,
probed, and signed, not just claimed.

## What it looks like in practice

A load-cell manufacturer models against OIML R 60: markings,
accuracy class, temperature behavior, creep — each mapped to the clause
that governs it. A customer importing that reference model gets your
conformity story as data they can compute against; a customer plugging
in the live twin gets **continuous** conformity — the compliance engine
watches the governed aspects and re-judges as evidence arrives.
Variants and family members share the model's common spine instead of
re-opening the file.

## The proof

The ACME LC-500 pilot walks the entire chain — manufacturer reference
model, customer import, live twin, twin certification — and the
simulator lets you rehearse every step before any hardware exists.
