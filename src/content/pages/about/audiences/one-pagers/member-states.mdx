<h1 class="one-pager-print-only one-pager-title">For Member States &amp; NMIs: the one-pager</h1>

<div class="one-pager-sheet">

**Your regulations cite the Recommendations; now your systems can read them.** OIML SMART turns the Recommendations into machine-actionable models with clause-level provenance: compliance becomes checkable, and the certificate register becomes something that answers. Your state keeps custody; the text of every Recommendation stays.

## What you do today

Your national type-approval regime references the Recommendations as PDFs. Every assessment is manual, every test report arrives unstructured, market surveillance samples instruments and hopes the sample tells the truth, and verifying a certificate means phoning its issuer.

## What changes

- **Verify any certificate your regime relies on, in seconds**, against the BIML-registered copy and the live revocation and suspension lists. No account, no phone call.
- **Read the register as the validity reference**: the registered copy is what verification answers from.
- **Read the Recommendations your regulation cites as models**: R 60's 180 requirements in 14 classes, 62 test procedures, each tracing to its clause.
- **Custody never moves**: the platform self-hosts under the Member State entitlement, with streaming updates; your designations and your legal responsibility stay exactly where your law puts them.

## Try it now

- Verify the demo certificate **R60/2021-A-EX1-26.01**: [demo.oimlsmart.org/app/verify](https://demo.oimlsmart.org/app/verify) (no account).
- Walk the live demo: [demo.oimlsmart.org](https://demo.oimlsmart.org/), one-click accounts, no registration; the instance resets nightly.
- Ask the OIML corpus with clause-level citations at the member tier: [ai.oimlsmart.org](https://ai.oimlsmart.org/).

## What you can use, and what you can run

The Member State category carries the estate's full entitlement: every hosted service, the self-host right for the platform software, the trust-registry registration. Quoted, never paraphrased, from the single source: www.oimlsmart.org/services/who-can-run-what.

## Who to talk to

info@oimlsmart.org. Onboarding starts with a conversation about which Recommendation first.
