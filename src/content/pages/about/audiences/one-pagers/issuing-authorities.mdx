<h1 class="one-pager-print-only one-pager-title">For Issuing Authorities: the one-pager</h1>

<div class="one-pager-sheet">

**Your certification workflow, run the way B 18 describes it.** Applications in, samples tracked, test work dispatched, evaluations synthesized, certificates signed: every verdict computed from the Recommendation's model rather than transcribed from prose. Your liability posture does not widen; the platform does digitally what your authority does today.

## What you do today

Applications arrive as scanned forms and emails; review happens in spreadsheets; samples move on paper custody chains; test reports come back as PDFs your officers re-type; the certificate is a signed PDF, and the register entry is a separate act nobody can query.

## What changes

- **Every application waiting on you, oldest first**, its age on the row; the reject is honest (a reason, recorded) and the accept opens the evaluation project.
- **The evaluation project is the shared dataspace**: samples, custody, dispatch, reports, one place, the applicant viewing what it may.
- **Verdicts computed from the model**, the decision still yours; the review period runs in the open.
- **Certificates as signed data (CNML)**, registered with the BIML; the public register is the validity reference.

## Try it now

- The Issuing Authority console: [demo.oimlsmart.org](https://demo.oimlsmart.org/), the one-click Issuing Authority account, then /app/ia. The review queue is yours.
- The intake flow, narrated step by step: www.oimlsmart.org/demo/ia-intake.

## What you can use, and what you can run

The Issuing Authority / Test Laboratory category (of a Member State), quoted, never paraphrased, from the single source: www.oimlsmart.org/services/who-can-run-what. An authority may also run its own instance (the IA-only posture); the deployment-mode matrix is at www.oimlsmart.org/use-cases/deployment-modes.

## Who to talk to

info@oimlsmart.org. Ask for a working visit against your own intake process; the pilot shape is one Recommendation, one authority, one laboratory.
