<h1 class="one-pager-print-only one-pager-title">For Test Laboratories: the one-pager</h1>

<div class="one-pager-sheet">

**Evidence captured once, at the moment of work.** The Recommendation's test procedures become a workbench your operators follow step by step; readings accrue as evidence with the operator and the timestamp attached; the test report is generated from that evidence, and your metrologists' time goes into measurement instead of paperwork mechanics.

## What you do today

Dispatches arrive by email; procedures are re-typed into bench software, one transcription per laboratory, each drifting a little from the Recommendation; the report is assembled by hand at the end, and the verdict re-check means reading the PDF against the clauses again.

## What changes

- **The dispatch lands in your inbox**: the request, the samples, the procedure the model declares.
- **The workbench walks the procedure step by step**: every step's purpose and clause read from the model, so two laboratories cannot run two different R 60s.
- **Readings become evidence at the bench**, with the operator and the timestamp; completeness is named, never silently absent.
- **The report is generated, never transcribed**, and where something is unprovable the record says indeterminate, honestly.

## Try it now

- The laboratory console: [demo.oimlsmart.org](https://demo.oimlsmart.org/), the one-click Test Laboratory account, then /app/lab.
- The laboratory's work, narrated step by step: www.oimlsmart.org/demo/tl-work.
- The TL-only pilot instance runs today: [tl.oimlsmart.org](https://tl.oimlsmart.org/) (the start-small posture).

## What you can use, and what you can run

The Issuing Authority / Test Laboratory category (of a Member State), quoted, never paraphrased, from the single source: www.oimlsmart.org/services/who-can-run-what. A laboratory may run its own instance (the TL-only posture); see www.oimlsmart.org/use-cases/deployment-modes.

## Who to talk to

info@oimlsmart.org. Ask to reproduce one of your past evaluations against the model; it is the fastest honest comparison.
