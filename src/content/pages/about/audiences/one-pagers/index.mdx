The leave-behind for the committee table: each of the seven audience
pages condensed to a single printable sheet, the same four answers on
every one (what you do today, what changes, what you can try now, who to
talk to), the entitlement line quoted from the single matrix source, and
the honesty footer naming the demo what it is.

**How to print.** Open a sheet and use your browser's print: the page is
styled to yield exactly one sheet (the site chrome and the screen-only
blocks hide; the sheet's own header prints). One per audience, seven in
the set; they are designed to be handed out together.

The sheets ride the [CIML tour](/tour): the presentation's last slide
hands them out, and the [presenter notes](/tour/notes) say when.

## The seven sheets

- [For Member States & NMIs](/about/audiences/one-pagers/member-states): custody never moves; the register answers; the full entitlement.
- [For Corresponding Members](/about/audiences/one-pagers/corresponding-members): everything hosted is yours today; the honest path upward.
- [For Issuing Authorities](/about/audiences/one-pagers/issuing-authorities): the OIML-CS as a digital workflow, on B 18:2025; liability unchanged.
- [For Test Laboratories](/about/audiences/one-pagers/laboratories): evidence captured once, at the moment of work; the report generated, never transcribed.
- [For Manufacturers](/about/audiences/one-pagers/manufacturers): apply once, follow it live, hold a certificate that answers for itself.
- [For Instrument Users](/about/audiences/one-pagers/instrument-users): stop trusting the sticker; start reading the record.
- [For Educators & Students](/about/audiences/one-pagers/educators): the classroom is the system itself.

Every sheet links back to its full audience page (the performed
capability inventory with the dated captures), so a reader who wants the
depth is one click from it.
