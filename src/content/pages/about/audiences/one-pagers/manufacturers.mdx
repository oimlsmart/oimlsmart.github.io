**Apply once. Follow it live. Hold a certificate that answers for itself.** You file against the Recommendation's model, watch every stage of the evaluation as it happens, and receive a certificate your customers and authorities verify on their own, any time, with no account and no phone call to anyone.

## What you do today

The application is a scanned form against an edition you hope is current; then silence until a letter arrives; the certificate is a PDF your customers cannot check without phoning the issuer, and every market asks for the same paperwork again.

## What changes

- **The application form is the Recommendation**: the instrument step renders the declaration the model authors; the submit gate names what is missing instead of refusing silently.
- **Every stage, as it happens**: notified at each, the application's page is the journey's spine; no more silence between the letter and the wait.
- **The certificate answers for itself**: anyone verifies it against the public register in seconds, no account, anywhere.
- **Less data entry over time**: a registered product reference can prefill the whole instrument chain.

## Try it now

- The applicant portal: demo.oimlsmart.org, the one-click Applicant account, then /app/portal. The new-application wizard is one click away.
- The application flow, narrated step by step: www.oimlsmart.org/demo/application.

## What you can use, and what you can run

The Applicant / public category, quoted, never paraphrased, from the single source: www.oimlsmart.org/services/who-can-run-what. Applying needs an account your organization approves; reading and verifying need nothing at all.

## Who to talk to

info@oimlsmart.org. Ask which Recommendations are modelled today (R 60, R 91, R 129, R 144) and what joining the pilot takes.
