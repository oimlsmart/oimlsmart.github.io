<h1 class="one-pager-print-only one-pager-title">For Instrument Users: the one-pager</h1>

<div class="one-pager-sheet">

**Stop trusting the sticker; start reading the record.** Any certificate presented to you verifies against the public register in seconds, with no account and no phone call. And if your organization is a Utilizer or an Associate under the OIML-CS, your designation is a registry fact and your national requirements become data the applicant sees before applying, not after.

## What you do today

A certificate is a PDF you take on trust or verify by phoning its issuer; designation scopes live in documents you request; your market's additional requirements surprise the manufacturer after the application, and everyone pays for the surprise.

## What changes

- **Verify any certificate by its number**: the answer comes from the BIML-registered copy and the live revocation and suspension lists, in seconds.
- **The register is public**: browse it, read the verdict basis, never trust letterhead again.
- **Your designation is a registry fact**: visible, scoped, current.
- **National requirements become data**: the applicant sees your market's additions before applying, so the evaluation covers them once.

## Try it now

- Verify the demo certificate **R60/2021-A-EX1-26.01**: [demo.oimlsmart.org/app/verify](https://demo.oimlsmart.org/app/verify) (no account).
- Read the public register: [demo.oimlsmart.org/app/register](https://demo.oimlsmart.org/app/register) (no account).

## What you can use, and what you can run

The Utilizer / Associate category, quoted, never paraphrased, from the single source: www.oimlsmart.org/services/who-can-run-what. Reading and verifying need no account at all.

## Who to talk to

info@oimlsmart.org. Ask how your organization's designation and national requirements would appear in the registry.
