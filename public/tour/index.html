The Digital OIML

OIML SMART turns legal-metrology standards into executable models, and runs the entire certification workflow on them — from a manufacturer's application to a digitally signed certificate anyone can verify.

This tour shows the working system, the deployment choices open to you, and the business opportunities each one creates.

The problem

Certification today is paper and correspondence

  • The certificate is a PDF: unverifiable without phone calls, unprocessable by software.
  • The standard is prose: every laboratory re-interprets it; every report is hand-assembled.
  • The workflow is email: applications, samples, reports, and decisions move by correspondence.
  • After issue, nothing watches: compliance is a point in time, not a state.
Keep this short. The audience knows the pain; the tour is the answer.
The idea

The standard becomes an executable model

Every Recommendation is authored in Primmel as a model of the instrument at its center — what it IS (identity, metadata, promises), what it HAS (attributes, dimensions, state), and what it DOES (behavior as processes with inputs, steps, and outcomes).

Requirements are constraints on those aspects. Conformance tests are operations over them. Test report forms are evidence models. The application, the lab work, the evaluation, and the certificate are all generated from — and judged against — that one model.

SMART Recommendations + Studio

Author once, execute everywhere

Experts author the Recommendation as a model in SMART Studio — processes, requirements, data classes, the works. The platform you will see in a moment runs on that exact model: the application form, the tests, the step-by-step lab instructions, the report forms, and the certificate content are all generated from it.

When the model changes, the platform changes. There is no second implementation to drift.

SMART Vocab

The vocabulary is digital and relational

The VIML (1968 to 2022) and the VIM (1984 to 2012) are digitalized as concept systems with relations (ISO 704, ISO 10241-1, ISO 12620) — searchable, browsable as concept diagrams, in English and French. The platform's models cite these concepts by reference, so the vocabulary is load-bearing, not decoration.

CNML

The certificate is a signed digital artifact

The Certificat Numérique de Métrologie Légale is the digital successor to the PDF certificate of conformity: signed at issuance with threshold PKI (a quorum signs, never a single key), verifiable by anyone, bound to its evaluation report, anchored in time, and revocable through a published CRL.

The same machinery signs measurements — the SMART+ direction: certified, machine-verifiable measurement streams from SMART Measuring Instruments.

The federation

Instances talk in signed packages

Nothing replicates. Data moves only when someone deliberately sends it — a test request to a laboratory, a test report back, a certificate registration to the CS — as signed, self-describing packages over declared endpoints, with correlation and receipts on both sides.

Trust is pinned: each instance publishes a descriptor with its endpoints and keys; peers pin each other; compromised keys are revoked federation-wide.

An offline path exists: download the package, deliver it by email, upload it at the peer — identical verification.
SMART Measuring Instruments + SST

The instrument itself can be a SMART node

A SMART Measuring Instrument serves a digital twin whose interface IS its Recommendation model — queryable, attested, certifiable. The OIML SST is the simulation environment for training on such instruments: physically faithful twins (creep, temperature, warm-up) that the platform's Test Process UI can drive and read during a test run.

Direction: certification OF and BY digital twin, continuous monitoring and continuous compliance, and CNML-signed measurements — the SMART+ umbrella.

The business opportunities

What this unlocks for members

  • NMIs — white-gloves evaluation services (engagement, quotation, agreement, the IA drives everything); your brand, your clients, your certificates registered with the CS.
  • Laboratories — receive work from any IA or NMI running the platform; your test reports are signed, structured evidence, not PDFs.
  • Manufacturers — one application, tracked end to end; a verifiable digital certificate your customers can check in a browser.
  • OIML-CS — the scheme runs on machine-readable rules with a public register and instant verification.
  • Everyone — a training environment (the SST) where operators learn on simulated SMART instruments before touching hardware.
